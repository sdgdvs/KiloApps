02. The Mathematics of 2-Operator FM Synthesis (YM2612) [1999-09-02]
Implementing Sega Genesis punchy slap bass and metallic bells on standard Sound Blaster hardware.

The Sega Genesis Yamaha YM2612 chip relies on frequency modulation rather than memory-heavy PCM sample banks. The fundamental mathematical equation for a 2-operator algorithm is:

s(t) = A_c(t) * sin( 2π * f_c * t + I_m(t) * sin( 2π * f_m * t ) )
