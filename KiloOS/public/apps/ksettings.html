⚙️ Welcome to KSettings

KSettings is your centralized Control Panel for KiloOS.

  • 🎨 Personalization: Customize system accent colors, desktop gradients, and wallpaper styles.
  • 🔊 Sound & Audio: Configure retro synth audio levels and preview system chimes.
  • 📌 Taskbar & Apps: Manage pinned shortcuts and quick-launch utilities.
  • 💾 Storage & VFS: Inspect virtual filesystem storage, export backups, or reset defaults.
  • ℹ️ System Specs: View 1999 environment metrics and live display diagnostics.

Shortcuts: [F1] Help | [F5] Quicksave | [F9] Quickload | [1-5] Switch Tabs | [Esc] Close Modals
