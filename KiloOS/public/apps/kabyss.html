Navigation & Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys / Numpad / Vi (hjklyubn): Move Delver across the grid.
  • Spacebar: Rest 1 turn to steady yourself (recuperates 1 HP/Sanity).
  • E / Enter: Interact with doors, stairs, chests, or altars.
  • S: Search adjacent tiles for hidden passages and traps.
  • Mouse Click: Click on visible floor tiles to move or inspect obstacles.

Field of View & Darkness

The Abyss is shrouded in primordial darkness. Your torch projects a field of light that illuminates corridors and chambers. Unexplored tiles remain pitch black; previously seen tiles remain remembered in faint gray memory.

Subterranean Hazards

Wandering deep crypts drains sanity. Keep your torch lit and avoid staying motionless in cursed chambers.
