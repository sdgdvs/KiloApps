Navigation & Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys / Numpad / Vi (hjklyubn): Move Delver across the grid.
  • Spacebar: Rest 1 turn to steady yourself (recuperates +2 HP, +1 Sanity, disallowed if starving).
  • E / Enter: Interact with doors, stairs, altars, cauldrons, or coffers.
  • T: Rekindle Torch via Pyre spell, Brimstone Ash, or standing next to flame!
  • B: Open the Ancient Alchemy Cauldron to brew potions and elixirs!
  • S / R: Search adjacent tiles for hidden passages, altars, and relic coffers.
  • Mouse Click: Click on visible floor tiles to move or inspect obstacles.

Survival Hazards: Sanity, Hunger & Occult Curses

  • Hunger Decay: Hunger drains every few turns. If hunger reaches 0, you starve and take HP damage each turn! Eat Food Rations (+45) or Crypt Mushrooms (+25).
  • Torch Burnout: Your torch burns down as you explore. When extinguished, your light radius drops to 1 tile and darkness sanity drain begins!
  • Pitch Darkness & Sanity: Navigating without light chips away at your Sanity. At low sanity, horrific eldritch whispers echo through the deep.
  • Cursed Effigies [!]: Sinister bone totems placed by cultists drain Sanity when delvers draw near. Bump into them to shatter them for essence, but beware of occult curses!
  • Occult Curses: Hexes such as Curse of Darkness, Enfeeblement, and Decaying Hunger afflict delvers. Purify them using Purifying Salt, Panacea, or Runic Altars.

Inventory & Equipment Slots [Tab 2 & Tab 1]

  • Delver's Pack [Tab 2]: Holds up to 16 items including potions, equipment, alchemy reagents, and keys. Filter by Potions, Gear, or Reagents.
  • Using Consumables: Click [Drink] on potions to restore Health, Sanity, Aether, or gain Ward Shields!
  • Equipment Slots [Tab 1]: Equip weapons (Runic Longsword, Voidfang Dagger), armor (Abyssal Mail, Shadowweave Cloak, Aegis Cuirass), relics (Torch of Eld, Aether Lantern, Radiant Censer), and amulets (Amulet of Vitality, Star Pendant, Void Eye) to amplify Might, Warding, Arcana, and Max stats!
  • Unequipping: Click any occupied equipment slot in Tab 1 to return the gear to your Delver's Pack.

Ancient Alchemy Cauldrons & Potion Brewing [B]

  • Cauldrons: Ancient iron cauldrons bubble with eldritch waters throughout the catacombs and grottos. Stand near one or press [B] anytime to brew!
  • Reagents: Harvest Blood Lotus, Azure Spores, Brimstone Ash, Void Dust, and Aether Blossoms from chests and defeated beasts.
  • Elixir of Vitality: Blood Lotus + Aether Blossom = +65 HP, +10 Sanity.
  • Draught of Lucid Mind: Azure Spores + Aether Blossom = +50 Sanity, +10 Aether.
  • Aether Phial: Void Dust + Aether Blossom = +40 Aether mana.
  • Stoneskin Brew: Azure Spores + Brimstone Ash = +40 Ward Shield.
  • Liquid Fire Flask: Brimstone Ash + Void Dust = 50 Fire AOE damage blast!
  • Panacea of the Deep: Blood Lotus + Brimstone Ash + Void Dust = +60 HP, +40 Sanity, +35 Aether!

Subterranean Depth Biomes

  • The Catacombs (B1 - B3): Traditional limestone crypts and stone corridors. Warm amber torches light ancient sarcophagi.
  • Sunken Grotto (B4 - B6): Cavernous flooded grottos with shallow water pools, stalagmites, and bioluminescent cyan fungi.
  • Forgotten Crypt (B7 - B9): Necrotic tomb labyrinth with blood-red skull braziers and ancient Runic Altars (press [E] to commune for blessings).
  • The Void Abyss (B10+): Shattered floating obsidian platforms suspended over bottomless cosmic chasms. Void rift crystals pulse with astral energy.

Relic & Ancient Rune Magic System

  • Z, X, V / Hotbar Clicks: Cast elemental spells from your equipped Staff Sockets!
  • Aether (Mana): Casting consumes Aether. Rest with [Space] or commune with Altars to restore Aether.
  • Rune Socketing [Tab 3]: Inscribe discovered runes into your staff sockets to change your active spells and passive bonuses.
  • ᚠ Pyre Blast: Seared ball of fire blasts 4 tiles forward, incinerating wooden doors and rubble.
  • ᚦ Glacial Nova: Freezes flooded water into solid ice walkways and protects with frost armor.
  • ᛋ Chain Bolt: Piercing electrical arc traveling 6 tiles forward, blasting open doors.
  • ᛟ Void Warp: Phase-shifts delver 3 tiles forward through obstacles or over bottomless chasms.
  • ᚨ Aegis Ward: Prismatic runic barrier absorbs up to 35 damage and restores +15 Sanity.

Abyssal Bestiary & Tactical Combat

  • Bump-to-Attack: Walk directly towards an adjacent monster to strike with your enchanted weapon or staff! Delver's Might and Arcana amplify melee blows.
  • 💀 Crypt Skeletons: Tireless undead that chase down intruders. Highly vulnerable to ᚠ Pyre Blast (+50% DMG). Drops Brimstone Ash & Blades.
  • 🦎 Mire Ghouls: Amphibious stalkers that lurk in flooded depths. Highly vulnerable to ᛋ Chain Bolt (+40% DMG). Drops Azure Spores.
  • 👻 Void Wraiths: Spectral horrors that phase through doors and rubble. Drains Sanity & Aether. Repelled by ᚨ Aegis Ward. Drops Void Dust.
  • 🧙 Crypt Acolytes: Necromancers hurling long-range necrotic Shadow Bolts. Rush them down in close melee! Drops Aether Blossoms.
  • 🐙 Abyssal Leviathans: Colossal titans with devastating crushing power. Encase them with ᚦ Glacial Nova (2-turn Freeze)! Drops Panacea & Aegis Armor.

Field of View & Darkness

The Abyss is shrouded in darkness. Your torch projects light across chambers and corridors. Light rays pass unobstructed through flooded waters and open cosmic chasms.
