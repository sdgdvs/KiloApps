Feature Overview

• Adjust & Presets: Real-time GPU/Canvas brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, and 8 cinematic color presets (Grayscale, Sepia, Invert, Vintage, Warm, Cool, Dramatic).

• Spatial Convolution Matrix: Professional 3x3 kernels (Sharpen, Unsharp Mask, Laplacian Edge, Sobel H/V, Emboss, Gaussian Blur, Ridge Detection) with custom weights, auto-divisor, and channel targeting.

• Transform & Interactive Crop: Lossless 90° rotations, horizontal/vertical flipping, and aspect-ratio constrained rectangle crop tool.

• Resize & Multi-Format Export: High-precision interpolation, aspect-locking, and export to PNG, JPEG, WEBP, and 32-bit BMP.

• Annotation Brush: Freehand vector drawing overlay with live color picker and variable brush radius.

• Inspector Panel: Subsampled multi-channel RGB luminance histogram & full EXIF camera metadata decoder.
