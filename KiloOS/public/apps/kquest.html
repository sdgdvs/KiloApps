KQuest Guide & Lore Codex

Comprehensive How-to-Play, Controls, Bestiary & Crafting Reference
🛡️ Hero Selection & Attributes

🗡️ Warrior Class

High HP & Defense. Excels at physical melee combat and absorbing damage. Abilities: Shield Bash & Iron Will barrier.

🔥 Mage Class

High Mana & Spell Power. Devastating elemental damage. Abilities: Fireball & Mana Surge cost discount.

🗡️ Rogue Class

High Speed & Critical Strike. Agile assassin. Abilities: Shadow Strike & evasion boosts.

🏰 Town Facilities & Gameplay Loop

🏰 Obsidian Spire Dungeon

Delve into 3 biomes (Mines F1-5, Catacombs F6-10, Dragon Spire F11-15). Encounter monsters, treasure chests, altars, and floor bosses.

🏟️ Boss Rush Arena

Battle 5 brutal boss waves in the Grand Colosseum. Earn Arena Trophies to unlock Legendary Artifacts (Conqueror's Blade, Runic Plate).

⚒️ Forge & Alchemy Bench

Salvage unwanted equipment into raw materials. Craft Fire Bombs, Greater HP potions, and Elixirs of Might. Imbue gear with elemental effects.

📜 Quest Board & Talent Hall

Accept daily bounties for XP & Gold. Allocate Skill Points into Offense, Defense, and Utility talent trees to unlock master passive buffs.

🛡️ Mercenary Guild

Recruit loyal companions: Paladin Tank (damage absorption), Archmage DPS (spells), or Cleric Healer (turn health regeneration).

💾 Save Slots & New Game+ (NG+)

Manage 4 persistent save slots. Defeat the final Spire Boss or conquer the Arena to activate NG+ for higher rewards and scaled foes!

⚔️ Combat & Environmental Hazards

Turn-Based Battles: Attack with equipped weapon, cast class spells, throw Fire Bombs or drink HP potions, command companion actions, or attempt to flee back to safety.

Environmental Hazards: Biomes feature ambient hazards (🪨 Cave-In in Mines, ☠️ Poison Fog in Catacombs, 🔥 Lava Burst in Spire) that deal damage each turn!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Battle Spells Reference

⚡ L Lightning Storm

In combat: Casts Lightning Storm (18 MP) dealing massive magic damage with 45% chance to DAZE target.

🛡️ S Holy Shield

In combat: Casts Holy Shield (14 MP) granting 2 turns 100% invulnerability barrier & +25 HP recovery.

🔥 P Phoenix Elixir

In or out of combat: Consumes Phoenix Elixir restoring 100% HP & MP (or auto-revives hero at 50% HP/MP upon defeat).

💥 B Berserk Might

In combat: Casts Berserk Might (12 MP) doubling damage output (+100%) for 3 turns.

📜 World Lore & History

📖 The Obsidian Spire Monolith

Erected thousands of years ago during the Great Cataclysm, the Obsidian Spire connects 15 dimensional realm chapters to dark elemental nexuses deep within the earth. Arcanist Malakor sealed his soul into the Void Citadel apex, seeking immortal dominance over all biomes.

⚙️ Material Sourcing & Salvaging

• ⚙️ Iron Scrap: Obtained from Mine & Catacomb foes, chest loot, or via Salvaging.

• ✨ Arcane Dust: Dropped by spellcasters, phantoms, or praying at blue dungeon altars.

• 🔥 Elemental Core: Harvested from volcanic beasts, elementals, and floor bosses.

• ♻️ Salvage Station: Pay 20 Gold at the Forge to break down spare loot into +2 Iron Scrap & +1 Arcane Dust.
