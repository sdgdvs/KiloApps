1. SHIP ARCHITECTURE & BLUEPRINT BAY

Welcome, Yardmaster. You oversee the deep-space fabrication facility StarForge IX. Every vessel begins on the 14x14 Blueprint Grid.

  • Bilateral Symmetry [S]: Ensures symmetrical mass and thruster balance. Highly recommended for warships and interceptors.
  • Command Bridge: Houses primary computer cores and pilot avionics. Every functioning ship requires at least one cockpit.
  • Hull Framing & Armor: Titanium plates absorb kinetic impacts; Reinforced Plating resists heavy railgun fire.

2. POWER GRIDS & THERMAL EQUILIBRIUM

Modern vessels generate and consume immense thermal and electrical loads:

  • Net Power (MW): Your reactors (Fission, Fusion, Antimatter) must generate more megawatts than your thrusters, shields, and weapons draw. Negative net power results in systems brownout.
  • Heat Dissipation: Active firing, maneuvering, and reactor coils create heat. Install sufficient Radiator Fins or Cryo-Coolers. If heat exceeds 100%, the reactor will trigger an emergency scram.

3. DRYDOCK ASSEMBLY & FABRICATION

Once your blueprint is engineered, submit it to the Drydock. Automated gantry cranes and welding laser drones will assemble the ship layer-by-layer using your yard's stockpile of Titanium, Ferrite, and Deuterium.

4. SHAKEDOWN PROVING GROUNDS

Take your constructed vessel on a shakedown cruise through the high-orbit asteroid proving grounds:

  • Flight Controls: [W/Up] Main Thruster, [S/Down] Retro-Brakes, [A/D/Left/Right] RCS Yaw, [Space] Fire Weapons, [E] Mining Beam, [Shift] Afterburners.
  • Asteroid Harvesting: Target mineral asteroids with your mining beam or cannons to extract raw ore into your cargo hold.
  • Syndicate Pirates: Rogue automated pirate drones patrol the outskirts. Neutralize them to earn yard bounties.

5. SHORTCUTS & SYSTEM COMMANDS

  • F5: Quicksave state to browser storage.
  • F9: Quickload last quicksave.
  • 1 - 5: Rapidly switch between views (Blueprint, Drydock, Proving Grounds, Contracts, Manual).
  • S: Toggle bilateral symmetry placement mode.
  • F1 / H: Open this Operations Manual.
  • ESC: Cancel selection, close modal, or return from flight range to drydock.
