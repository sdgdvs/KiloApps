    How to Play KWords - Loop 7 Edition

    Goal: Find all hidden target words and secret bonus words in the grid!

    • Words can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal (forwards or backwards).
    • Click and drag to select a word in the grid.

    Campaign & Modes

    • Campaign Mode: 20 progressive stages with dynamic grid dimensions (10x10 to 20x20), 12 themes, stage timers, frozen tiles, fog of war, and Stage 20 Polyglot Grandmaster Challenge!
    • Classic / Time Attack / Zen: Play custom difficulties.

    Active Skills & Assistance (Hotkeys)

    • Word Radar (R / W): Sonar pulse highlights start letter of target word.
    • Word Pathfinder (P): Outlines full exact path of 1 target word.
    • Freeze Timer (F): Freezes countdown timer for 15 seconds!
    • Hint (H): Reveals a random hidden letter in target word.

    Obstacles & Features

    • Frozen Tiles: Cyan ice tiles require adjacent solves or 2 direct clicks to thaw!
    • Fog of War: Dark cells hide letters until revealed by search or skills.
    • Bonus Secret Words: Hidden bonus words award +500 PTS!
