📖 KPomodoro Quick Start Guide

⏳

1. The 25/5 Cadence

Work with undivided focus for 25 minutes, followed by a 5-minute break. After 4 focus sessions, enjoy an extended 15-minute long break to avoid mental fatigue.

🎯

2. Single-Task Intentionality

Pin a specific task before you hit Start. Break big goals into estimated pomodoro chunks (🍅) and mark them done as you finish.

🎧

3. Procedural Ambience

Activate synthesized focus soundscapes: Analog clock ticking, 432Hz binaural alpha brainwave drones, or calming rainstorm.

⚡

4. Workstation Ergonomics

Use hotkeys for speed: Space (Start/Pause), 1-3 (Phase Select), S (Skip), R (Reset), H / F1 (Help Guide), F5 (Quicksave), F9 (Quickload), and Esc to dismiss dialogs.

⚙️ Quick Preferences

Cycle durations and sound alerts can also be configured anytime under the "Cycle Settings" tab on the right desk.
