Detective's Manual

How to Play

1. Move between locations and Search for Clues (Costs 2h).

2. Analyze objects in the Evidence Lab to reveal usable clues (Costs 1h).

3. Interrogate suspects by presenting clues to catch them in lies (Costs 1h).

4. Accuse the killer with the correct motive and weapon before time runs out!

Interrogation Tips

- Every suspect has a breaking point (Patience). Presenting unrelated clues reduces patience.

- Presenting the killer's specific item will catch them in a lie immediately!

- Cross-reference alibis! If someone's alibi contradicts another's, they might be lying.

Evidence Guide

- In the lab, calibrate the scanner waveform to match the target frequency.

- Use the +11, +7, and -3 frequency shifts strategically.
