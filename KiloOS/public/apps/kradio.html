📻 KRadio Player Guide

KRadio streams live synthwave, ambient, lo-fi, and internet radio stations in real time inside KiloOS with HiDPI retro spectrum visualization.

Space / P Toggle Play / Pause stream
S Stop audio stream
F5 Quicksave active station & playback state
F9 Quickload restored session & playback
1 – 6 Quick tune station preset
← / → Cycle previous / next preset
M Mute / Unmute audio output
↑ / ↓ Adjust volume up / down (+/- 5%)
V Cycle visualizer style (LED / CRT / VU)
Enter Tune to custom URL in text box
F1 / ? / H Toggle this Help guide
Esc / Space / Enter Dismiss Help guide

Tip: Direct HTTPS MP3 and AAC streaming endpoints are supported. Custom streams can be tuned anytime.
