📻 KRadio Player Guide

KRadio streams retro, ambient, electronic, and internet radio stations in real time directly inside KiloOS.

Space / P Toggle Play / Pause
S Stop audio stream
1 – 6 Select Station Preset
M Mute / Unmute audio
↑ / ↓ Increase / Decrease volume
Enter Tune to custom URL in text box
F1 / ? / H Toggle this Help guide
Esc Dismiss Help modal

Tip: Direct HTTPS MP3 and AAC stream links are fully supported.
