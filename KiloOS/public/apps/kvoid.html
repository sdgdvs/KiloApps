Survival Guide (Press H to close)

Controls & How to Play

WASD / Arrows: Move
Space: Use EMP (Stuns nearby aliens)
H: Toggle Help
Survive, find the elevator to the next deck. Watch your Oxygen and Battery. If either hits zero, you die.

Lore Index

You are trapped on a derelict station. The crew was experimenting on alien lifeforms... it didn't go well. You must escape before life support fails completely or they find you.

Enemy Bestiary

The Entities: Sensitive to noise and movement. If they catch you, it's instant death. They glow magenta. Hide in Lockers to avoid detection.
Stunned Entities: Glow blue. Safe to pass temporarily.
