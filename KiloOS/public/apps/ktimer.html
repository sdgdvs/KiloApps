🧭 Modes & Features

⏱️ Stopwatch: High-precision millisecond timing with split lap calculation, fastest/slowest badges, and CSV / TXT report exports.

⏳ Countdown Timer: Custom countdown with 1m–60m quick presets, custom saved presets, and 5 synthesizer sound alerts.

🎛️ Multi-Timer: Run multiple labeled timers simultaneously with individual progress bars and batch Start/Pause.

🍅 Pomodoro: Focus intervals (25m Work, 5m Short Break, 15m Long Break) with automatic session and daily streak tracking.

🏋️ Interval / HIIT: Circuit timer with customizable Prep, Work, Rest durations, and Tabata/HIIT/Boxing presets.
