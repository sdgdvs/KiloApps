🎓 KClip Quick-Start Tutorial

Welcome to KClip! Here is how to master your clipboard workflow:

  • 📋 Capture & Paste: Press Capture Clip or paste directly into KClip with Ctrl+V. You can also type quick clips in the top bar.
  • ⭐ Pin Protection: Click the pin button or press P on any clip. Pinned clips are permanently preserved when clearing unpinned history.
  • ⚡ Real-time Transforms: Use the Transform bar to instantly convert between UPPERCASE, lowercase, JSON formatting, Base64, and URL encoding.
  • ⚡ Quick Snippets: Access pre-made templates (ISO timestamps, GUIDs, C/HTML boilerplate) via the Snippets button.
  • 💾 Sovereign Persistence: Press F5 anytime to Quicksave or F9 to Quickload. Your state is preserved in local sovereign storage.
