KConnect4 - Loop 7

Rules: Connect 4 discs vertically, horizontally, or diagonally!

Campaign Mode: 20 Stages with dynamic grid sizes (7x6 to 10x8) and 4 AI Personalities (Rookie, Aggressive, Trapper, Grandmaster Minimax)!

Special Discs:

  • Bomb (1/B): Explodes 3x3 surrounding cells.
  • Drill/Anvil (2/D): Crushes cell directly underneath.
  • Magnet (3/M): Pulls friendly discs from adjacent columns.

Active Skills:

  • AI Hint (H): Highlights best move column.
  • Undo Move (U): Reverts last turn pair.
  • Column Freeze (F): Freezes opponent column for 2 turns.
