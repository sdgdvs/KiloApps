How to Play

Controls: Arrow keys, WASD, or swipe to slide tiles.

Active Skills / Hotkeys:
U: Tile Upgrade (Doubles lowest tile)
R: Grid Rotate (Rotates grid 90° clockwise)
H: Hammer Smasher (Destroys 1 unwanted tile)
Z / Ctrl+Z: Free Undo (Reverts last move)
N: New Game / Restart
C: Campaign Mode (30 Stages)
M: Time Attack Toggle
P: Auto-Play Toggle
F: Cycle Ruleset
T: Cycle Theme
3, 4, 5, 6: Change Grid Dimensions

Special Tile Mechanics:
❄ Frozen Tiles: Must be merged TWICE to thaw and clear.
💣 Bomb Tiles: Merging explodes 3x3 surrounding cells.
⭐ Wildcard Gems: Merges with any tile to upgrade it.
✖ Stone Obstacles: Impassable blocker blocks.
