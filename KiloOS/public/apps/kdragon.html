Minigames & Sub-Controls

  • Strength Power Meter: Press Space or Enter to strike at peak gauge.
  • Speed Reflex Test: Press Space or Enter immediately when signaled.
  • Loyalty Cup Guess: Press 1, 2, or 3 to choose box.
  • Battle Arena: 1 Attack, 2 Defend, 3 Special, 4 Flee.
  • Shop Items: 1 Meat, 2 Toy, 3 Bracer, 4 Boots.

Stats & Evolution

  • Hunger / Happiness / Energy: Core needs. Keep them above 0!
  • Strength (Str): Increases battle strike power and minigame rewards.
  • Speed (Spd): Increases hit rate and evasion against enemies.
  • Loyalty (Loy): Amplifies special healing and elemental power.
  • Evolution at Age 10:
    • Mostly Fed → Earth Dragon (Earthquake slow)
    • Mostly Played → Fire Dragon (Devastating Fireball)
    • Mostly Slept → Water Dragon (Healing Stream)
    • Trained Rigorously (Str & Spd ≥ 10 or Loy ≥ 12) → Astral Dragon (Starfall cosmic strike)
  • Elemental Affinities:
    • Fire deals +40% vs Frost/Ice beasts.
    • Water deals +40% vs Fire/Magma drakes.
    • Earth deals +30% vs Physical/Ground foes.
    • Astral deals +50% vs Shadow creatures.
  • Sacred Hoard Relics: Collect all 6 rare artifacts on expeditions to achieve Treasure Sovereign status!
