Dragon Master's Guide

How to Play

Raise your dragon from an egg. Keep it fed, happy, and well-rested. As it grows, it will evolve into a mighty adult with a specific elemental affinity based on how you raised it.

Stats Explanation

  • Hunger/Energy/Happiness: Core needs. Don't let them reach 0!
  • Strength (Str): Increases attack power in battles and minigames.
  • Speed (Spd): Increases hit chance and dodge rate.
  • Loyalty (Loy): Affects special abilities like Healing Stream.
  • Age: At age 10, baby dragons evolve.

Evolution

  • Mostly Fed: Earth Dragon
  • Mostly Played: Fire Dragon
  • Mostly Slept: Water Dragon

Item Codex

  • Meat: Restores 50 Hunger, 20 Energy.
  • Toy: Restores 50 Happiness, 10 Energy.
  • Bracer: Permanently grants +5 Strength.
  • Boots: Permanently grants +5 Speed.
