OPERATOR MANUAL: KANOMALY v1.0.0

1. Operational Overview

KAnomaly is a subterranean acoustic and VLF radio signal analyzer designed to detect, filter, and decode anomalous transmissions pulsating through the Earth's lithosphere.

2. Receiver Controls & Tuning

- Borehole Station Selector: Switch between 6 global subterranean observatories (Kola, Carlsbad, Mariana, Yamantau, Hadron, Atacama). Each station taps into distinct geological strata and ground noise levels.
- Frequency Dial: Tune from 1.00 kHz up to 120.00 kHz in fine (+/-0.1k) and coarse (+/-1k) increments.
- Bandwidth Filter: Narrow (50Hz), Medium (250Hz), Wide (1kHz), Ultra (4kHz). Narrower filters maximize SNR on weak harmonic carriers.
- Demodulator Modes: RAW/AM, FSK telemetry, CW Morse code, SSTV / Spectral Raster, and Hex Stream.

3. Subterranean Telemetry & Classified Signals

12 subterranean anomalies exist within the lithospheric static. Decoded packets reveal darknet node relays (e.g. 10.19.99.4 from Carlsbad Bunker 0x7F) and anomalous clock cycles (FLEET_CYCLE_1999). When cataloging anomalies, use the Triangulation Map to pinpoint the epicenter!
