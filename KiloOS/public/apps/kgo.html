How to Play KGo

Goal: Control more territory (empty grid intersections) than your opponent.

📜 Core Rules & Mechanics

  • Alternating Turns: Black plays first, then White. Click any empty intersection to place a stone.
  • Liberties & Capture: Stones stay alive as long as they have at least 1 adjacent empty intersection (liberty). When all liberties of a group are surrounded, it is captured and removed.
  • Superko Rule: Recreating ANY previous board position in the match is strictly prohibited.
  • Suicide Rule: Placing a stone with 0 liberties is illegal unless that move captures opposing stones.
  • Komi & Scoring: Total Score = Controlled Territory + Captured Stones + Komi (White bonus, 6.5 in even games).
  • Game Over: Two consecutive passes by both players trigger score calculation.

💡 Strategic Assistance Engines

  • Hint (H): Grandmaster AI evaluates and highlights the optimal candidate move with score.
  • Liberty Analyzer (S): Highlights stone groups and overlays numbered badges on all remaining liberties.
  • Territory Estimator (T): Displays colored territory markers and flashes warning borders on groups in Atari (1 liberty).
  • Undo (U / Ctrl+Z): Reverts last turn (move pair against AI, single move locally).
