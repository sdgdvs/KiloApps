How to Play KGo

Goal: Control more territory (empty intersections) than your opponent.

  • Liberties & Capture: Stones must have at least 1 empty adjacent point (liberty). If surrounded, they are captured.
  • Superko Rule: Recreating ANY previous board state in game history is strictly prohibited.
  • Suicide Rule: Cannot place a stone with 0 liberties unless it captures opponent stones.
  • Scoring: Territory + Captures + Komi (White bonus).
