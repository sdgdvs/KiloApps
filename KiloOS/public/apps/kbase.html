💡 Feature Highlights
  • Multi-Base Live Converter: Real-time 64-bit synchronization between Binary (0b), Octal (0o), Decimal, Hex (0x), Custom base (2-36), and ASCII/Unicode characters.
  • 64-Bit Toggle Grid & Ops: Interactive bit manipulation board with shift, rotate (ROL/ROR), reverse, byte swap (endianness), and bitwise AND/OR/XOR/NOT/SAR operations.
  • IEEE-754 Inspector: Visual single (32-bit float) and double (64-bit double) breakdown with color-coded Sign, Exponent, and Mantissa bits and exact reconstruction formulas.
  • Varint & Bitfields: LEB128 / SLEB128 / Protobuf ZigZag encoder/decoder, multi-width (int8..64) two's complement inspector, and bitfield slicer with Popcount/CLZ/CTZ diagnostics.
  • Export & History: Automatic calculation logging with instant CSV and JSON export.
