🧪 Core Alchemy Loop

Welcome, Apprentice! Your goal is to uncover all 56 hidden elements across 5 alchemical tiers by combining reagents in your lab crucible.

Select Slot 1 and Slot 2 from your Grimoire on the left panel.
Click ✨ Transmute Elements to perform synthesis.
If successful, a new element will be permanently unlocked and added to your Grimoire!
Failure yields Slag or Smoke while granting +5 Arcane Dust.
🔬 Laboratory Equipment Navigation
🫕 Crucible: Primary 2-element synthesis chamber.
⚗️ Retort: Purifies elements into Arcane Essence & Dust.
🧪 Alembic: Distills potent magic reagents.
🔨 Anvil: Crushes high-tier items back to base components.
📜 Quests: Fulfill Guild orders for Gold & Guild XP.
🧙 Workshop: Upgrade lab tools & unlock Auto-Sorter.
🥣 Potions: Brew elixirs for temporary yield & XP boosts.
📖 Codex: Inspect detailed recipes, lore & craft metrics.
⚡ Challenge Game Modes
🧪 Classic Mode: Unrestricted sandbox discovery and lab progression.
⚡ Timed Blitz: 60-second speed challenge! Discover compounds before time expires to build high scores.
🧩 Puzzle Crucible: Synthesize specific target elements in minimum moves for extra rewards.
🌊 Tier 1 — Primordial Basic Elements

Elements (4): Fire 🔥, Water 💧, Earth 🌍, Air 💨

The fundamental building blocks of creation. Available from the start of your apprenticeship.

🌿 Tier 2 — Nature & Raw Resources

Elements (10): Steam ♨️, Mud 💩, Lava 🌋, Dust 🌫️, Energy ⚡, Rain 🌧️, Plant 🌿, Metal ⚙️, Pressure 🗜️, Stone 🪨

Crafted directly by combining two Primordial T1 elements. Forms natural phenomena and mineral deposits.

🛡️ Tier 3 — Metallurgy & Crafting Reagents

Elements (14): Glass 🥛, Steel 🛡️, Life 🧬, Lightning 🌩️, Firestorm 🌪️, Clay 🏺, Charcoal 🪵, Cloud ☁️, etc.

Forged through intense heat, biological crystallization, and industrial processing.

🔮 Tier 4 — Arcane Transmutations

Elements (16): Golem 🗿, Electricity 🔋, Crystal 💎, Magma Orb 🔮, Phoenix 🐦‍羽, Mana Core 🧿, etc.

High-tier enchanted compounds requiring rare T2 & T3 ingredients and alchemical precision.

🌟 Tier 5 — Celestial Apex Artifacts

Elements (12): Philosopher's Stone 💎✨, Elixir of Life 🧪💫, Celestial Star 🌟, Primal Void 🌌, Cosmarch Orb 🪐, etc.

The pinnacle of transmutation. Forged only by Master Alchemists possessing ancient recipes.

🖱️ Mouse & UI Controls
Click Element Card: Auto-loads the element into the active Crucible slot.
Click Crucible Slot: Clears the selected element from that slot.
⚡ Auto-Fill Button: (Unlocked via Workshop Auto-Sorter Lvl 1+) Scans your Grimoire and automatically places valid missing recipe pairs into the Crucible!
🔮 Oracle & Research: Spend Arcane Dust to request a Vague Hint (20 Dust) or Oracle Vision (50 Dust).
🔊 Audio Toggle: Click header badge to mute or restore laboratory sound effects.
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
1 - 5 Switch Tier Filters (T1 - T5)

📜 The Dawn of the Great Synthesis

Before kingdoms arose and magic was codified into spells, the primordial cosmos existed as a swirling vortex of raw chaos. Four Great Spirits channeled the chaos into Fire, Water, Earth, and Air — establishing the physical foundation of the world.

🏰 The Guild of Grandmaster Alchemists

Founded centuries ago in the Citadel of Aethelgard, the Guild seeks to unlock the secrets of universal transfiguration. Guild members dedicate their lives to mapping the 56 Sacred Reagents and constructing the ultimate catalyst: The Philosopher's Stone.

⚖️ The Law of Equivalent Harmonics

Matter is never destroyed in the Crucible. When two elements combine under harmonic resonance, their molecular structures realign, elevating the compound into a higher tier of power while shedding residual Arcane Dust.

🌟 Secrets of Apex Alchemy

Grandmaster alchemists whisper of Tier 5 Celestial Reagents — elements so pure that they transcend physical matter, granting infinite life, boundless essence, and mastery over space and time.
