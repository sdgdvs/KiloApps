How to Play KHangman

Rules: Guess the hidden word letter by letter before your strikes run out.

Game Modes:

  • Freeplay: Classic Hangman mode with custom or chosen categories.
  • Campaign Mode: 20 stages across 10 categories (4-14 letters, 3-6 strikes) ending in Stage 20 Polymath Grandmaster!
  • Time Attack Blitz: 60s timer! +2s per correct letter, +15s per word solved!
  • ⚔️ Online Duel (RFMS): Real-time cross-computer 1v1 battle! Race to solve the word first. Includes 25s auto-fallback to CyberBot-99!

Active Skills & Power-Ups:

  • [1] Vowel Reveal: Instantly reveals 1 hidden vowel in target word.
  • [2] Consonant Radar: Reveals 1 hidden consonant.
  • [3] Strike Shield: Absorbs 1 incorrect letter guess strike.
  • [4] Freeze Timer: Freezes stage/blitz timer for 15 seconds.
  • [5] Bomb Nuke: Eliminates 3 incorrect letters from keyboard.
  • [F2] Hint: Displays a diegetic, non-spoiling contextual clue.

Keyboard Shortcuts:

  • A - Z: Guess letter on keyboard
  • 1 - 5: Activate skills (1: Vowel, 2: Radar, 3: Shield, 4: Freeze, 5: Bomb)
  • F2 / Alt+H: Toggle word clue / hint
  • F5 / F9: Quicksave / Quickload game state
  • Space / Enter: Restart game or advance to next campaign stage
  • F1 / ?: Help modal & tutorial (Escape to close any modal)
