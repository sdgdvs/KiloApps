How to Play KHangman

Rules: Guess the hidden word letter by letter before your strikes run out.

Game Modes:

  • Freeplay: Classic Hangman mode with custom or chosen categories.
  • Campaign Mode: 20 stages across 10 categories (4-14 letters, 3-6 strikes) ending in Stage 20 Polymath Grandmaster Challenge!
  • Time Attack Blitz: 60s timer! +2s per correct letter, +15s per word solved!

Active Skills & Power-Ups:

  • Vowel Reveal [V]: Instantly reveals 1 hidden vowel in target word.
  • Consonant Radar [H]: Reveals 1 hidden consonant.
  • Strike Shield [S]: Absorbs 1 incorrect letter guess strike.
  • Freeze Timer [F]: Freezes stage/blitz timer for 15 seconds.
  • Bomb Nuke [B]: Eliminates 3 incorrect letters from keyboard.
