📋 Welcome to Task Manager

Inspect live active applications, monitor CPU/RAM metrics, and manage runtime tasks across KiloOS.

⚡
Process List: Filter by title or PID, and click headers to sort ascending/descending.
🛑

🚀
New Task: Launch programs with Ctrl+N or the toolbar button.
