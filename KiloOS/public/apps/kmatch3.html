How to Play KMatch3

Swap adjacent gems to form lines of 3 or more of the same color.

Special Gems:
- Match 4: Line Blaster (destroys row or column).
- Match 5: Rainbow Gem (destroys all gems of selected color).
- T / L Match: 3x3 Bomb Gem (explodes 3x3 area).

Obstacles & Boss:
- Ice Tiles: Cannot be moved. Break by matching internal gem.
- Stone/Iron Tiles: Shatter in 1 to 3 hits.
- Stage 20 Boss: Jewel King (75 HP, Barrier Gems, Moving Obstacles!).

Game Modes:
- Campaign (20 Stages): Dynamic 6x6 to 10x10 boards & objectives.
- Zen: Infinite relaxing play.
- Timed Rush: Speed challenge against the clock.
- ⚔️ Duel Arena: Real-time Match-3 duel vs online peers or GemBot-99 AI! Combos send Ice and Stone attacks.

Active Skills (Cost 300 Score):
- [H] Hammer: Destroy any single gem, ice, stone, or iron tile.
- [E] +Moves / +15s: Add extra moves or timer seconds.
- [S] Shuffle: Rearrange all board gems.
- [L] Color Nuke: Nuke all gems of target color.

Shortcuts & State Management:
- [F5]: Quicksave State snapshot.
- [F9]: Quickload State snapshot.
- [R]: Watch move replay viewer.
- [Esc]: Deselect or close modals.
