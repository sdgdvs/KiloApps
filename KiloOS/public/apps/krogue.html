⌨️ KEYBINDINGS & CONTROLS
📖 HOW TO PLAY KROGUE

Goal: Descend through 50 floors into the Caverns of Chaos to defeat True Astaroth!

Movement: Use Arrow Keys or Numpad (8/2/4/6) to move. Press [.] or [Space] or Numpad 5 to wait a turn.

Combat: Walk into an adjacent enemy to strike with your weapon. Bows target with [F].

Class Abilities: Press [A] to trigger your active class power (e.g. Berserk Slash, Arcane Nova, Shadow Step).

Spellbook: Press [M] to view and cast known magic spells (Mage starts with Magic Missile & Heal).

Inventory & Gear: Press [I] to manage carried items, quaff potions, equip weapons/armor, and eat food.

Enchanting & Sockets: Press [E] or step onto an Altar to infuse elemental gems (Ruby=Fire, Sapphire=Ice, Emerald=Venom, Amethyst=Void Siphon, Topaz=Shock) into gear sockets!

Companion Pets: Press [P] or discover Pet Nests to recruit loyal pets (Wolf Pup, Arcane Wisp, Stone Golem, Phoenix) that assist in combat and grant perks!

Challenge Vaults: Enter Astral Vault Portals on secret floors to challenge gauntlets, defeat guardian bosses, and claim game-changing Relics!

Key Rebinding: Press [K] to customize controls to your personal setup.

Quicksave / Quickload: Press [F5] or [S] to save run, [F9] or [L] to resume.

Run History / Export: Press [T] for the Leaderboard and JSON Export/Import.

Shortcut: Press [H] or [F1] anytime to open this guide.
