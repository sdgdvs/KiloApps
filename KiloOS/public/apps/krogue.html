📖 HOW TO PLAY KROGUE

Goal: Descend through 50 floors to slay True Astaroth!

Movement: Use Arrow Keys / WASD / Custom Keybinds to move/attack. Press [.] or Numpad 5 to wait a turn.

Combat: Bump into enemies to attack. Ranged weapons require targeting [F].

Classes & Magic: Each class has a unique ability [A]. Mages start with spells [M].

Custom Keybinds: Press [K] to rebind controls to your liking.

Run Stats JSON: Export/Import your leaderboard JSON on the [T] Leaderboard screen.
