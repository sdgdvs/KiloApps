Farmer's Almanac

How to Play:
Click a grass tile to Till it.
Select a seed and click tilled soil to Plant.
Click a planted seed to Water it (do this daily!).
Click a fully grown crop to Harvest and earn money.

Crops:
🌾 Wheat: Grow 2d, Base Val $10 (Mill: $25), Spring/Fall
🌽 Corn: Grow 3d, Base Val $20, Summer
🍅 Tomato: Grow 4d, Base Val $30, Summer/Fall
🎃 Pumpkin: Grow 5d, Base Val $50, Fall

Animals:
🐔 Chicken: $5/day (Mayo Maker: $15/day)
🐄 Cow: $15/day (Cheese Press: $40/day)

Weather:
☀️ Clear: Need 1 water
🌧️ Rain: Auto-waters crops
🏜️ Drought: Need 2 water
🐦 Crows: Can eat crops (Buy a Scarecrow!)

Keyboard Shortcuts:
[1] - [4]: Select Seed (Wheat, Corn, Tomato, Pumpkin)
[Space]: Sleep / Next Day
[H] or [F1]: Toggle Farmer's Almanac
[Esc]: Close Almanac Dialog
