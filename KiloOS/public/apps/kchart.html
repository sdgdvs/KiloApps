📊 KChart Studio — User Guide & Controls
🚀 1. Quick-Start 4-Step Tutorial
  1. Choose or Load Data: Pick a preset from the dropdown (or press P), drag & drop a CSV/JSON file, or edit labels and values in the Data Table.
  2. Select Visualization Engine: Press 1–0 or click the toolbar buttons to switch between 10 visualization modes: Bar, Line, Area, Pie, Donut, Radar, Horizontal Bar, Stepped Waveform, Scatter Plot, and Polar Coxcomb Rose.
  3. Analyze Trends & Regressions: Press T to cycle overlays: Linear OLS Fit ($y=mx+b$), 2nd-Order Quadratic Polynomial ($y=ax^2+bx+c$), Exponential Fit ($y=ae^{bx}$), Moving Average, Mean Baseline, and Confidence Corridor ($\pm\sigma$).
  4. Sonify & Export: Press U to hear the dataset sonified with the Sega Genesis YM2612 FM Sound Chip & SNES SPC700 stereo delay echo, and export vector SVG, raster PNG, Markdown table, C code array, standalone HTML, CSV, or JSON.
📊 2. Visualization Modes (10 Engines)

Switch between 10 distinct visualization rendering engines: Bar Chart [1], Line Graph [2], Area Gradient [3], Pie Chart [4], Doughnut Chart [5], Radar / Spider Chart [6], Horizontal Bar [7], Stepped Waveform [8], Scatter Plot [9], and Polar Area / Coxcomb Rose [0]. Hover over any point, bar, slice, or petal for interactive tooltips.

📈 3. Mathematical Regressions & Curve Fitting

Overlay advanced mathematical models onto charts (Press 'T' to cycle):

  • Linear Fit: Ordinary Least Squares ($y = mx + b$) regression with slope and $R^2$ goodness-of-fit.
  • Polynomial Quadratic Fit: Exact 2nd-order parabola ($y = ax^2 + bx + c$) solved via matrix Cramer's rule.
  • Exponential Fit: Log-linearized growth/decay curve ($y = a \cdot e^{bx}$).
  • Moving Average: 3-point rolling window smoothed trend curve.
  • Mean Baseline: Arithmetic dataset mean reference line.
  • Confidence Corridor: Shaded $\pm 1\sigma$ standard deviation band and extrema envelope.

🎵 4. Sega Genesis YM2612 & SNES SPC700 Data Sonification

Experience your data through sound! KChart Studio incorporates a procedural Sega Genesis (Yamaha YM2612 2-operator FM synthesis) and Super Nintendo (SPC700 stereo delay warmth) sound engine. Press U to sonify your data: an animated playhead sweeps across the canvas while each data point is synthesized as a musical pitch mapped to its magnitude.

🧮 5. Data Operations & Benchmarks

Execute one-click mathematical transformations on your dataset: Normalize (0–100%), Cumulative Sum ($\Sigma$), Discrete Differential ($\Delta$), Gaussian Smoothing, and Reverse, with full Undo (Z) support. Set a custom Benchmark / Target Line to evaluate KPI performance in real time.
