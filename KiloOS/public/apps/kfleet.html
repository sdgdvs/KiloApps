The lower center scope traces real-time telemetry waveforms. Toggle Channel A (Throughput kbps), Channel B (Signal RSSI), Channel C (Buffer fill %), and Channel D (Entropy index). Use [Space] to pause/freeze the live trace for detailed analysis.
