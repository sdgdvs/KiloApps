Subnetting & CIDR Rules

Subnetting allows isolating departments into separate broadcast domains. In a /24 subnet (mask 255.255.255.0), 8 bits remain for host IDs, yielding 256 addresses (254 usable, reserving .0 for Network ID and .255 for Broadcast).
