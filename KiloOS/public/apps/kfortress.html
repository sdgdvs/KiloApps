COMMANDER'S FIELD GUIDE

📖 HOW TO PLAY

Defend your Castle Fortress from enemy waves! Build towers on empty slots (+) using Gold. Earn Gold by defeating enemies and clearing waves. Protect your Base HP; if it reaches 0, you lose.

🏰 TOWER SPEC SHEET

  • 🏹 Archer (50g): Fast firing, medium range. Good vs Goblins. Upgrades scale well.
  • 🔮 Mage (100g): Medium fire rate. Deals Splash (AoE) damage. Excellent vs swarms.
  • 💣 Cannon (150g): Slow fire rate, huge single-target damage. Best vs armored Orcs & Ogres.
  • ❄️ Frost (120g): No damage, but creates a freezing aura that slows all enemies by 60% inside its range.

👹 ENEMY BESTIARY

  • 👺 Goblin: Fast, low HP. Basic swarm unit.
  • 👹 Orc: Slow, high HP. Armored (takes 50% damage from Archers). Use Cannons or Magic!
  • 🐺 Hound: Very fast, low HP. Slips past slow towers easily.
  • 🦇 Gargoyle: Flying unit. Bypasses ground pathing and heads straight for the castle!
  • 🧌 Ogre Boss: Massive HP, slow. Deals 5 damage to base. Grants huge gold bounty.

🛡️ TACTICAL STRATEGIES

  • Paladin Hero: Move your hero to intercept enemies. Use Shield Wall (2) right before enemies hit the castle to prevent base damage!
  • Traps: Place Spike Pits on corners. Use Barricades to stall Ogres.
  • Synergy: Place a Frost Tower near Cannons so enemies stay in range longer.
