COMMANDER'S FIELD GUIDE - LOOP 2

🌟 ELEMENTAL TOWER FUSIONS (MAX LEVEL 3 -> FUSION)

When any tower reaches Level 3, click it again to fuse into a Mythic Elemental Fusion Tower for 220g:

  • 🌋 Inferno Mortar (Mage / Cannon): Blazing magma mortars that ignite lingering lava puddles melting all enemy ranks.
  • 🌩️ Superconductor (Tesla / Frost): High-voltage cryo-electric storms arcing to 5 targets with 65% freeze slow.
  • ☣️ Venomspite Piercer (Poison / Ballista): Heavy plague javelins piercing entire enemy lines with corrosive blight DoT.
  • ☀️ Solar Prism Beam (Archer): High-frequency radiant photon laser that ramps damage against bosses and titans.

🏰 CASTLE SIEGE DEFENSES & TREBUCHET (HOTKEY 5)

  • Siege Trebuchet (5): Calldown massive siege boulder bombardment anywhere on the map for 220 area damage.
  • Castle Auto-Ballistas: Research "Siege Weaponry" in the Academy to mount automated defense ballistas on the fortress.

⚡ CHALLENGE MUTATORS (M)

  • Bloodlust: +40% enemy speed, +50% gold reward.
  • Titan Brood: +100% enemy HP, 2x boss spawns, +100% score bonus.
  • Arcane Eclipse: Hero cooldowns +40%, Towers gain +30% attack speed.
  • Meteor Tempest: Sky meteor strikes periodically rain down on the battlefield.
  • Phase Shift: Enemies have 25% phase dodge chance vs standard attacks, +75% gold reward.
