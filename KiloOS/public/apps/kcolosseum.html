      Arena Combat

      Crowd Favor: 0%

      Lanista's Guide

      HOW TO PLAY:
      Buy gladiators from the market, equip them, and train their stats.
      When ready, send them to the Arena to fight and earn Denarii.
      If your gladiator dies, they are lost forever! Heal them between fights.

      COMBAT TACTICS:

      • Attack: Uses STR for damage. Hit chance is based on AGI difference.
      • Defend: Skips your turn, but drastically reduces enemy hit chance and damage.
      • Showboat: Skips your turn to build Crowd Favor. At 100%, the crowd rewards you with gold or healing!
      • Flee: Saves your gladiator's life, but you drop an Arena Level in disgrace.

      EQUIPMENT REFERENCE:

      • Gladius (Glad): +3 STR (More damage)
      • Trident (Trid): +3 AGI (Higher hit & dodge chance)
      • Armor (Armr): +5 VIT (+50 Max HP)
      • Shield (Shld): Greatly increases effectiveness of the Defend command.
