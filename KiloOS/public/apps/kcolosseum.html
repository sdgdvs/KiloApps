      Lanista's Guide

      HOW TO PLAY:
      Recruit fighters from the slave market, train their physical attributes, and equip legendary gladiatorial armaments. When ready, dispatch them into the sands of the Arena to win prestige, crowd favor, and Denarii.
      Beware: If your gladiator falls in battle, they are lost forever to the Underworld! Heal your fighters between bouts.

      KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:

      • F5 / F9: Quicksave / Quickload Ludus snapshot
      • F1 / H / ?: Open / close this Lanista's Guide
      • O: Open Colosseum Online Duels
      • C: Open Combat Replay & Chronicles
      • T: Open Ludus Hall of Fame & Statistics
      • M: Toggle audio sound effects on/off
      • R: Refresh recruit market (50D)
      • 1, 2, 3: Quick-recruit market fighter 1, 2, or 3
      • Space / Enter: Attack in combat / Enter arena with ready fighter / Dismiss modals
      • A / 1: Attack in arena combat
      • D / 2: Defend in arena combat
      • S / 3: Showboat for Crowd Favor in combat
      • W: War Cry / Rally in online & cyber duels
      • F / 4: Flee combat (retreat with gladiator's life intact)
      • Esc: Dismiss open dialogs or exit arena after battle

      COMBAT TACTICS:

      • Attack: STR scales damage. Hit chance scales with AGI advantage.
      • Defend: Dramatically minimizes enemy hit rate and damage taken.
      • Showboat: Gathers crowd adoration. At 100% Crowd Favor, gold showers down or doctors toss healing sponges!
      • Flee: Preserves your veteran gladiator's life at the price of ludus arena standing.

      EQUIPMENT & WEAPON MASTERY:

      • Gladius: +3 STR; 20% arterial rend chance (+4 bonus bleed damage).
      • Trident: +3 AGI; 20% entanglement chance (cripples foe's next strike by 50%).
      • Twin Sica: +3 STR, +4 AGI; 28% Twin-Blade Flurry chance (secondary strike) & cleaves through defense.
      • Cuirass Armor: +5 VIT (+50 Max HP) with bronze muscle plate.
      • Bronze Shield: Enhanced block mitigation plus devastating counter Shield Bash on enemy misses.
      • Bare Fists: Brawler's Fury grants double Crowd Favor gain per landed hit.

      ARENA LEGENDS & SPECIAL BOSSES:

      • Ferocious Lion: Fast, savage beast with bleeding claws and high agility.
      • Armed Chariot: High-momentum war horse and scythed wheel carriage with devastating charges.
      • Twin Gladiators: Dual-fighter tag team executing coordinated flanking strikes.
      • Gallic Behemoth: Colossal barbarian titan with spiked war maul and earth-shattering slams.
      • Praetorian Champion (Level 6+): Imperial elite in gilded lorica squamata with purple cape, Roman scutum bulwark, and spatha thrusts. Grants massive Denarii and +35 Favor!
      • Thracian Executioner (Level 7+): Legendary Dimachaerus wielding twin curved Sica blades. Features rapid Twin-Blade Flurries, cleaves through shield defenses, and yields huge Denarii (+180D) and +40 Favor!

      🏛️ WELCOME TO ROME, LANISTA

      You have taken ownership of a fledgling Gladiator Ludus in 1999 B.C. Rome. The Emperor and the Roman mob crave spectacle, courage, and blood.

      Your Path to Glory:

      1. Recruit: Scout promising fighters from the market on the left.
      2. Arm & Train: Equip swords, tridents, armor, and shields. Train their STR, AGI, and VIT.
      3. Fight in the Arena: Send seasoned fighters to the pit. Earn Denarii and advance your Ludus to the Grand Colosseum!
      4. Protect Your Veterans: If wounded, heal them before the next match. Death in the arena is permanent.
