OVERSEER SURVIVAL HANDBOOK

WELCOME, OVERSEER. Your directive is to sustain Vault 704 and shepherd its survivors through the nuclear fallout.


1. RESOURCE BALANCING:

• Food & Water: Each citizen consumes rations daily based on your Overseer Directives. If reserves hit zero, survivors starve, lose morale, and suffer radiation/sickness.

• Power: Facilities require electrical power from the Generator Room. If power load exceeds generation, brownouts will disable non-essential facilities.

• Scrap: Gathered by Scavenger teams in the Wasteland or fabricated in the Workshop. Needed for repairs and room expansion.


2. WORKER ALLOCATION:

Assign your unassigned citizens to production facilities (Hydroponics, Purifiers, Generators). Facilities with assigned workers produce more resources each day cycle!


3. THE DAY CYCLE:

Press [ADVANCE DAY CYCLE] (or hit SPACEBAR) to process 1 full day of consumption, production, recovery, and wasteland encounters.


4. RETRO-TERMINAL CRT THEMES & AESTHETICS:

Switch between authentic post-apocalyptic display themes using the THEME button or press [T]: Amber CRT (Phosphor P3), Wasteland Rust (Scorched Iron), Phosphor Green (P1), or Monochrome (P4 Radar). Toggle CRT raster scanlines with [C].


5. ROOM CONSTRUCTION & FACILITY EXPANSION:

• Upgrades: Upgrade existing facilities up to Level 3 using Tech Scrap. Upgrades boost output (power, food, water, scrap), unlock extra staff worker slots, and enlarge shelter citizen capacity!

• Construction: Switch to the CONSTRUCT ROOMS & EXPANSION sub-tab to excavate new specialized chambers: Auxiliary Bio-Turbines, Deep Well Purifiers, Aeroponic Green Bays, Barracks Extensions (+6 Cap), Turret Bastions (+15 Defense), and Scrap Smelters!


6. SURVIVOR RECRUITMENT & ATTRIBUTE ASSIGNMENT:

• Attribute Alignment: Matching survivor attributes to their work station boosts efficiency (up to 150%+). Agility & Strength benefit Farms; Intelligence benefits Water Purifiers, Medical, and Command; Strength & Intelligence power Generators; Strength powers Scrap Workshops and Defense Turrets.

• Radio Recruitment Beacon: Switch to the RADIO BEACON & AIRLOCK sub-tab to pulse distress pings or broadcast specialist beacons to attract new survivors across the wasteland. Review candidates and admit them into the vault if quarters capacity allows.


7. WASTELAND RUINS & BLUEPRINT DISCOVERY:

• Exploration Runs: Send unassigned citizens out past the airlock into ruins (Supermarkets, Power Substations, Hospital Wings, Military Armories, and Vault 811).

• Medical Supplies (Meds): Scavenge sterile pharmaceuticals and stimpacks to heal wounded dwellers (-1 Med restores +35 HP) or equip scouts before high-hazard sorties.

• Lost Blueprints: Scouts with high Intelligence can decrypt encrypted archives in ruins to recover exclusive facility blueprints (Fusion Micro-Reactor, Automated Surgery Wing, Rad-Shield Airlock Gate, Hydroponic Gene-Vault) and construct them in Engineering!
