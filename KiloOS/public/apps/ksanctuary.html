OVERSEER SURVIVAL HANDBOOK

WELCOME, OVERSEER. Your directive is to sustain Vault 704 and shepherd its survivors through the nuclear fallout.


1. RESOURCE BALANCING:

• Food & Water: Each citizen consumes rations daily based on your Overseer Directives. If reserves hit zero, survivors starve, lose morale, and suffer radiation/sickness.

• Power: Facilities require electrical power from the Generator Room. If power load exceeds generation, brownouts will disable non-essential facilities.

• Scrap: Gathered by Scavenger teams in the Wasteland or fabricated in the Workshop. Needed for repairs and room expansion.


2. WORKER ALLOCATION:

Assign your unassigned citizens to production facilities (Hydroponics, Purifiers, Generators). Facilities with assigned workers produce more resources each day cycle!


3. THE DAY CYCLE:

Press [ADVANCE DAY CYCLE] (or hit SPACEBAR) to process 1 full day of consumption, production, recovery, and wasteland encounters.


4. RETRO-TERMINAL CRT THEMES & AESTHETICS:

Switch between authentic post-apocalyptic display themes using the THEME button or press [T]: Amber CRT (Phosphor P3), Wasteland Rust (Scorched Iron), Phosphor Green (P1), or Monochrome (P4 Radar). Toggle CRT raster scanlines with [C].
