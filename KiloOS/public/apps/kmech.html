- Swarm Cluster Missiles: Multi-warhead missile salvo (34 ATK, 42 Heat, explosive collateral splash).

TACTICAL COMBAT & SUBSYSTEM TARGETING:
- Attack: Deals weapon damage and builds up core heat. Exceeding Max Heat causes 15 HP catastrophic burnout!
- Defend: Doubles DEF armor, vents reactor heat (bonus active cooldown), and activates Repair Nanodrones if equipped.
- Head/Cockpit: Precision strike (20% hit chance, 3.0x lethal damage, stuns enemy systems).
- Torso: Center mass (80% hit chance, 1.0x damage, highest reliability).
- L.Arm: Shield/Subsystem disarm (60% hit chance, 1.4x damage, strips 2 enemy DEF).
- R.Arm: Weapon disarm (60% hit chance, 1.4x damage, reduces enemy ATK by 3).
- Legs: Mobility strike (55% hit chance, 1.2x damage, grants +15% evasion next turn).

- Repair Nanodrones: Restores +10 HP (scaling with rank) whenever defending / venting heat.
