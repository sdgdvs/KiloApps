TACTICAL COMBAT & HEAT:
- Attack: Deals weapon damage and raises your mech's heat. Exceeding Max Heat causes severe 15 HP core burnout damage!
- Defend: Doubles DEF plating and cools your reactors without generating heat.
- Targeting: Head deals 3.0x lethal damage with lower accuracy. Torso offers safe, reliable 1.0x hits. Limbs offer 1.5x damage.

PARTS & PILOT UPGRADES:
- XP & Leveling: Each pilot level gives +5% accuracy and evasion boost.
- Jump Jets: +20% evasion bonus.
- Energy Shield: Absorbs up to 5 damage per incoming hit.
