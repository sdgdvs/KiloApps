TACTICAL COMBAT & SUBSYSTEM TARGETING:
- Attack: Deals weapon damage and builds up core heat. Exceeding Max Heat causes 15 HP catastrophic burnout!
- Defend: Doubles DEF armor, vents reactor heat, and activates Repair Nanodrones if equipped.
- Head/Cockpit: Precision strike (20% hit chance, 3.0x lethal damage, stuns enemy systems).
- Torso: Center mass (80% hit chance, 1.0x damage, highest reliability).
- L.Arm: Shield/Subsystem disarm (60% hit chance, 1.4x damage, strips 2 enemy DEF).
- R.Arm: Weapon disarm (60% hit chance, 1.4x damage, reduces enemy ATK by 3).
- Legs: Mobility strike (55% hit chance, 1.2x damage, grants +15% evasion next turn).

HOSTILE MECH TIERS:
- Battles 1-2: Scout Raider (Agile raptor chassis, rapid pulse laser).
- Battles 3-4: Assault Goliath (Heavy armor, shoulder quad-missile pod).
- Battles 5-6: Stealth Interceptor (Swept delta chassis, optical active-camo).
- Battles 7-8: Siege Titan (Quad-leg walking fortress, heavy siege mortars).
- Battles 9+: Apex Overlord (Command dreadnought with void cyclops eye and forcefield).

PILOT RANKS & SUBSYSTEMS:
- XP & Pilot Ranks: Cadet → Scout → Veteran → Ace → Commander (+5% accuracy & evasion per level).
- Jump Jets: +20% evasion bonus.
- Energy Shield: Absorbs 6 damage per incoming hit.
- Overdrive Core: +25% attack damage boost (adds +12 heat).
- Repair Nanodrones: Restores +10 HP whenever defending / venting heat.
