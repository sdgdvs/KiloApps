Key Features
  • Single Convert: Instant bi-directional unit conversion with live formula breakdown.
  • Batch Mode: Simultaneously view conversions across all units in the selected category.
  • Smart Parser: Evaluate natural language strings like 100 km/h to m/s or 50 psi to bar.
  • Favorites: Pin frequent conversion pairs with ⭐ for 1-click loading.
  • Custom Units: Add proprietary or custom engineering scale factors.
  • History Log: Automatic audit trail with export to CSV/JSON.
Supported Physical Categories

Length (m, km, cm, mm, mi, yd, ft, in, nmi), Weight (kg, g, mg, t, lb, oz, st), Temperature (°C, °F, K), Data (B, KB, MB, GB, TB, PB), Speed (m/s, km/h, mph, knot, ft/s), Area (m², km², ft², acre, ha), Volume (L, mL, m³, gal, qt, fl oz), Time (sec, min, hr, day, wk, yr), Pressure (Pa, kPa, bar, psi, atm, mmHg).
