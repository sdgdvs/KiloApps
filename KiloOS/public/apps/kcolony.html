KColony Administrator's Manual

How to Play

Manage your colony's resources: Food, Power, Materials (Mat), and Advanced Materials (AdvM). Expand to support more population (Pop) and keep them happy (Hap). Research tech to unlock advanced structures.

Structure Spec Sheet

  • Solar Panel (S): Generates 4 Power during the day.
  • Farm (F): Produces 5 Food per tick. Costs 5 Power.
  • Mine (M): Produces 3 Mat per tick. Costs 10 Power.
  • Habitat (H): Increases Max Pop by 5. Costs 5 Power.
  • Battery (B): Increases Max Power by 50. Stores power for night.
  • Laboratory (L): Produces 2 Science per tick. Costs 5 Power.
  • Nuclear Reactor (N): Generates 20 Power constantly.
  • Hydroponics (Y): Produces 15 Food per tick. Costs 10 Power.
  • Laser Defense (D): Range 5. Costs 20 Power. Automatically shoots aliens.
  • Wall (W): Defensive structure to slow down aliens.
  • Turret (T): Range 3. Costs 5 Power. Shoots aliens.
  • Factory (C): Converts 2 Mat into 1 AdvM per tick. Costs 10 Power.

Tech Tree Guide

1. Hydroponics (50 Sci): Unlocks highly efficient food production.
2. Factory (75 Sci): Unlocks production of Advanced Materials.
3. Nuclear Reactor (100 Sci): Unlocks massive 24/7 power generation.
4. Laser Defense (150 Sci): Unlocks long-range anti-alien defenses.

Disaster Survival Strategies

- Meteor Showers & Breakdowns: Have excess Mat (5 per repair) ready to fix damaged structures.
- Dust Storms: Solar efficiency drops. Build batteries to survive power outages, or rely on Nuclear Reactors.
- Alien Attacks: Build walls to absorb hits and place Turrets/Lasers nearby to neutralize the threat quickly.
