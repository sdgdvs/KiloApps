KColony Administrator's Manual & Expanded Spec Sheet

Resource Systems

Food: Colonist nourishment. Power: Energy network. Mat: Basic structural alloy. AdvM: High-tech alloy. Science: Research breakthroughs. Happiness: Productivity multiplier (up to 120%).

Core Structures

  • Solar Panel (S): Generates 4 Power in daylight. (+50% with Solar IV).
  • Farm (F): +5 Food per tick. Costs 5 Power.
  • Mine (M): +3 Mat per tick. Costs 10 Power.
  • Habitat (H): +5 Max Population. Costs 5 Power.
  • Battery (B): +50 Max Stored Power for night/storms.
  • Laboratory (L): +2 Science per tick. Costs 5 Power.
  • Nuclear Reactor (N): +20 Power 24/7 constantly.
  • Hydroponics (Y): +15 Food per tick. Costs 10 Power.
  • Laser Defense (D): Range 5. Costs 20 Power. Auto-targets aliens.
  • Wall (W): Fortification. (With Xeno-Armor, reflects 2 damage to attackers).
  • Turret (T): Range 3. Costs 5 Power. Auto-targets aliens.
  • Factory (C): Converts 2 Mat into 1 AdvM per tick. Costs 10 Power.

New Advanced Structures (Loop 2)

  • Geothermal Generator (G): +35 Power constantly (immune to dust storms/blizzards; +50 Power on Cryo world). Cost: 35 Mat, 15 AdvM.
  • Bio-Dome / Terraformer (V): +25 Food, +8 Pop cap, +2 Happiness per tick up to 120%. Cost: 40 Mat, 20 AdvM, 20 Power.
  • Shield Pylon (E): 3x3 Energy Barrier protecting structures from meteors, blizzards, acid rain, and repels alien invasions. Cost: 50 Mat, 25 AdvM, 30 Power.
  • Drone Hub (U): Automated nanite repair drones fix broken structures on the grid (5 Mat) and boost nearby production. Cost: 60 Mat, 30 AdvM, 25 Power.

Expeditions & Mutator Anomalies

- Scout Recon (1 Pop, 15 Mat, 15 Pwr): Low risk survey for raw materials and terrain data.
- Ruins Excavation (2 Pop, 30 Mat, 30 Pwr): High rewards; chance to unlock Crystal Monolith (+50% Science).
- Hive Incursion (4 Pop, 60 Mat, 50 Pwr): Strike alien brood nest; unlocks Subterranean Geode (Mines yield AdvM) or Ionized Aurora (+50 Max Power)!
