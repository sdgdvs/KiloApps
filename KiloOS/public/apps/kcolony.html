Resource Systems

Food: Colonist nourishment. Power: Energy network. Mat: Basic structural alloy. AdvM: High-tech alloy. Science: Research breakthroughs. Happiness: Productivity multiplier (up to 120%).

Core Structures

  • Solar Panel (1 / S): Generates 4 Power in daylight. (+50% with Solar IV).
  • Farm (2 / F): +5 Food per tick. Costs 5 Power.
  • Mine (3 / M): +3 Mat per tick. Costs 10 Power.
  • Habitat (4 / H): +5 Max Population. Costs 5 Power.
  • Battery (5 / B): +50 Max Stored Power for night/storms.
  • Laboratory (6 / L): +2 Science per tick. Costs 5 Power.
  • Nuclear Reactor (7 / N): +20 Power 24/7 constantly.
  • Hydroponics (8 / Y): +15 Food per tick. Costs 10 Power.
  • Laser Defense (9 / D): Range 5. Costs 20 Power. Auto-targets aliens.
  • Wall (10 / W): Fortification. (With Xeno-Armor, reflects 2 damage to attackers).
  • Turret (11 / T): Range 3. Costs 5 Power. Auto-targets aliens.
  • Factory (12 / C): Converts 2 Mat into 1 AdvM per tick. Costs 10 Power.

Advanced & Expansion Structures (Loop 2 & 3)

  • Geothermal Generator (13 / G): +35 Power constantly (immune to dust storms/blizzards; +50 Power on Cryo world). Cost: 35 Mat, 15 AdvM.
  • Bio-Dome / Terraformer (14 / V): +25 Food, +8 Pop cap, +2 Happiness per tick up to 120%. Cost: 40 Mat, 20 AdvM, 20 Power.
  • Shield Pylon (15 / E): 3x3 Energy Barrier protecting structures from meteors, blizzards, acid rain, and repels alien invasions. Cost: 50 Mat, 25 AdvM, 30 Power.
  • Drone Hub (16 / U): Automated nanite repair drones fix broken structures on the grid (5 Mat) and boost nearby production. Cost: 60 Mat, 30 AdvM, 25 Power.
  • Trade Port (17 / P): +10 Happiness, +3 Mat & +1 AdvM tariffs. Interstellar freighters arrive every 4 days! Cost: 70 Mat, 35 AdvM, 30 Power.
  • Cavern Drill (18 / K): +40 Power deep magma tap, +5 Mat & +2 AdvM 24/7. 100% immune to surface disasters! Cost: 80 Mat, 40 AdvM, 40 Power.
  • Orbital Beacon (19 / O): 100% planetary defense umbrella against meteors and unlocks tactical ORBITAL BOMBARDMENT! Cost: 100 Mat, 50 AdvM, 50 Power.

Expeditions & Operations

- Scout Recon (1 Pop, 15 Mat, 15 Pwr): Low risk survey for raw materials and terrain data.
- Ruins Excavation (2 Pop, 30 Mat, 30 Pwr): High rewards; chance to unlock Crystal Monolith (+50% Science).
- Hive Incursion (4 Pop, 60 Mat, 50 Pwr): Strike alien brood nest; unlocks Subterranean Geode or Ionized Aurora!
- Freighter Trade: Exchange surplus Food or Mat for AdvM, Science, and emergency power.
- Cavern Dive: Excavate deep subterranean crystal vaults for massive rare alloy caches.
- Orbital Strike [Space]: Calls down kinetic bombardment from orbit to vaporize all aliens instantly!
