📖 KVault Help & Security Guide

Welcome to KVault! Your secure, client-side encrypted password manager, TOTP authenticator, and confidential data storage.

  • Encryption: Local AES-GCM 256-bit encryption with PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds). The Master Password is NEVER sent over the network or stored unencrypted.
  • Multi-Vault Profiles: Press [F6] to switch between Primary Vault, Work & Ops, and Classified Mesh. Each profile maintains an isolated encrypted storage compartment.
  • Cipher Workbench & Hasher: Press [Ctrl+H / F4] to open the on-demand cryptographic workbench for AES-GCM, XOR-Stream-1999 (Win32 interoperable), SHA-256, and SHA-1 checksums.
  • Schema Presets & Builder: Apply pre-built schemas for Web Login, Credit Cards, Server/SSH, API Keys, Passkeys/Seeds, 1999 BBS, Database, or Router credentials.
  • Tree Explorer & Preview: Press [Alt+2 / T] to browse items hierarchically by Compartment and Category with an instant document & credential preview pane. Press [Alt+1] for Card Grid view.
  • TOTP 2FA Authenticator: Stores RFC 6238 Base32 secret keys and calculates live 6-digit one-time authorization tokens refreshed every 30 seconds with animated timers.
  • Drag & Drop Vault Import: Drag any .json, .csv, .xml, .asc, or .dat archive onto the window with visual focus glow to import or restore secrets.
  • Password Generator Studio: Press [Ctrl+G] anytime for high-entropy random passwords, 1999 Diceware passphrases, or PINs.
  • Security Audit: Click 🛡️ Audit to assess overall vault health, detect weak or reused credentials, and identify missing 2FA.
  • Quicksave & Quickload: Press F5 to save an instant snapshot of your vault state to localStorage, and F9 to restore it.
  • Auto-Lock & Wipe: Automatically locks and clears the operating system clipboard after inactivity.
  • Shortcuts: [Alt+1] Grid View, [Alt+2 / T] Tree Explorer, [↑/↓] Navigate Tree, [/] Jump to Search, [F4 / Ctrl+H] Cipher Workbench, [F5] Quicksave, [F6] Switch Profile, [F9] Quickload, [F1 / H] Help, [M] Audio, [Ctrl+S] Save/Export, [Ctrl+L] Lock, [Ctrl+F] Find, [Ctrl+G] Generator, [Esc] Clear search / Close modals.
