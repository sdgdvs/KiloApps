HOW TO PLAY KSNAKE

🐍 Movement: WASD or Arrow keys. On touchscreens, swipe or tap grid.

⚡ Skills: [G] Ghost (glide through walls/tail), [F] Freeze (slow enemies 50%), [M] Magnet (pull fruit).

🍎 Fruits: Red (+pts/grow), Gold (+500pts), Poison (-200pts), Speed (+300pts & 1.5x score), Ghost (+300pts). Beware oil & magma!

💾 Quicksave: [F5] Quicksave, [F9] Quickload anytime. [P] Pause, [S] Save & Exit.

🎮 Modes: Classic, Maze, Ramp (accelerates per apple), Wrap, Campaign (30 stages + bosses), VS (CPU rivals), Custom (Editor), Gauntlet.
