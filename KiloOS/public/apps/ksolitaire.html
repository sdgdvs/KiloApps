📖 How to Play & Rules
Objective
Build all 4 foundation piles up by suit from Ace to King (A, 2, 3 ... K). In the 7 tableau columns, build down in alternating red/black suits.
Game Modes
• Classic Solitaire: Traditional scoring (+10 foundation, +5 tableau card flip, -15 back to tableau).
• Vegas Money Mode: Start with -$52 buy-in. Earn +$5 per foundation card (-$5 if returned to tableau). Your bankroll persists across matches!
• Campaign Mode (20 Stages): Progressive challenge campaign featuring Suit-Locked foundations, Frozen ice cards, and stock recycling limits.
Active Skills (3 uses per match)
🪄 [W] Magic Wand: Automatically moves 1 valid card from waste or tableau to foundation.
👁️ [X] X-Ray Vision: Temporarily reveals all face-down tableau cards for 5 seconds.
🔀 [S] Shuffle Stock: Shuffles all remaining stock and waste cards together.
Controls & Shortcuts
• Left-Click: Select and move cards between columns or foundations.
• Double-Click / Right-Click: Fast auto-move card to foundation.
• [Space] / [D]: Draw card from stock pile / recycle stock.
• [F1] / [?]: Open/Close Help | [H] / [Ctrl+H]: Smart Hint
• [F2] / [N]: New Game | [F5 / Ctrl+S]: Quick Save | [F9]: Quick Load
• [F3]: Select Deal Number | [F4]: Replay Studio | [F6]: Solitaire Duel Arena
• [1-3]: Modes (Classic, Vegas, Campaign)
• [W] Wand: Move card | [X] X-Ray: Reveal | [S]: Shuffle Stock
• [U] / [Ctrl+Z]: Undo | [Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z]: Redo | [Ctrl+F]: Auto-Finish
• [M]: Toggle Audio Mute | [T]: Cycle Card Deck Theme
• [Arrow Keys]: Navigate columns and foundation piles | [Enter/Space]: Select/Move
• [Esc]: Close Modals / Victory Banner, Clear Selection, Dismiss Toast.
