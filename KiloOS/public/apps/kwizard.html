Grimoire & How to Play

How to Play

You and your opponent take turns casting spells. You start with 1 Max Mana, gaining 1 per turn (up to 10). Your mana refreshes at the start of your turn. Draw a card each turn.

Defeat the opponent by reducing their HP to 0. Build your deck to create powerful synergies!

Status Effects

  • Shield: Absorbs incoming damage.
  • Burn: Take damage at the start of your turn.
  • Poison: Take damage at the start of your turn.
  • Frozen: Skip your next turn or cannot cast spells this turn. Ice Lance does 3x damage to frozen targets!
  • Regen: Heal HP at the start of your turn.
