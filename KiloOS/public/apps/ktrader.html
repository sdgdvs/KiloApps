Captain's Log - Help Guide

How to Play

Travel between planets to buy low and sell high. Watch your fuel and be prepared for random encounters like space pirates, asteroid fields, and Navy patrols. Complete missions from the Mission Board to earn extra credits.

Trade Goods Reference

  • Food & Water: Cheap at Agri planets, expensive elsewhere.
  • Ore: Cheap at Mining planets, needed by Industrial/Tech.
  • Tech & Meds: Produced at Tech planets, needed by others.
  • Luxury / Contraband / Military: High profit, but requires reputation with Traders (50+), Pirates (50+), or Navy (50+). Contraband may be confiscated by Navy patrols!

Ship Upgrades

  • Cargo Bay: Increases max cargo capacity, also bolsters energy shields (+10 per level).
  • Engine Drive: Reduces hyperspace fuel consumption per jump.
  • Weapons: Increases laser damage against pirate raiders.
  • Dreadnought: Ultimate 100,000 credit flagship purchase for total galaxy supremacy.

Flight Controls & Hotkeys

  • [1-9]: Jump to connected planetary destination routes
  • [C]: Open 12-System Galactic Star Chart & Jump-Gate Topology
  • [U] / [G]: Open Commodity Futures Ledger / Syndicate Multiplayer Duel
  • [R]: Quick refuel ship at starport / emergency solar recharge
  • Trading: Use Buy Max to quickly purchase all units you can afford and hold in cargo hold; Sell All to quickly offload inventory.
  • [F5] / [F9]: Quicksave / Quickload voyage state
  • Export / Import: Save / restore JSON voyage backups
  • [F1] / [H] / [?]: Open / Close this Captain's Log
  • [Esc]: Dismiss dialogs & close modals
  • Combat: [Space / F] Fire lasers | [E] Evasive flee | [B] Bribe pirates
