How to Play Freecell (Loop 7 Expansion)

Rules:

  • Build all 4 foundations from Ace to King by suit.
  • Move cards between tableau columns (Alt colors or Baker's Suit rules).
  • Use Free Cells at the top left to store single cards. Extra cells can be unlocked temporarily.
  • Moving multiple cards requires enough empty free cells/columns.
  • Some campaign stages restrict empty tableau slots to Kings only or contain Frozen cards.
  • Frozen cards thaw when exposed at column top or when adjacent plays occur.

Active Skills & Assistance:

  • [W] Magic Wand: Auto-detects and plays 1 optimal safe card to Foundation or Freecell.
  • [E] Extra Cell: Unlocks +1 temporary Free Cell slot for 30 seconds!
  • [A] Auto-Solve: Continuously moves safe cards to Foundations.
  • [U] / [Z] Free Undo: Unlimited move undo.
  • [P] Shuffle: Shuffles remaining tableau cards in place.

Modes:

  • Random / Numbered Deal.
  • Campaign Mode: 20 stages featuring 4-cell, 3-cell, 2-cell constraints, Baker's rules, King-only spaces, Frozen cards, and Stage 20 Grandmaster Challenge.
  • Time Attack: 180s countdown timer! Foundations add +15s bonus time.
