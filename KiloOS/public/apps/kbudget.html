Features & Usability Tips

• Interactive Categories: Click any category badge in the transaction list or legend to instantly filter entries.

• Quicksave & Quickload: Store and restore complete workstation snapshots (F5 / F9).

• CSV & JSON: Export ledgers to CSV for spreadsheets or JSON for complete backups.

• Expenses Pie Chart: Real-time category allocation with sharp HiDPI rendering.

• Balance Projection Graph: Switch views [P] to forecast forward 30-day and 60-day balances based on net burn rate and budget pace.

Welcome to KBudget

KBudget is your personal ledger, expense tracker, and real-time financial visualizer.

Quick Start:

  • Click + New Transaction or press Ctrl+N to add income or expenses.
  • Press F5 anytime to quicksave a snapshot of your entire ledger.
  • Press F9 to restore your quicksaved state.
  • Press F1 or H for the full keyboard shortcuts and user guide.
  • Import/Export spreadsheets using the Data ▾ menu.
