What is KContribute?
Inspired by SETI@home, Folding@home, and early distributed volunteer computing projects, KContribute allows anyone to donate free Google Gemini quota to autonomously build, test, and polish apps in the KiloApps ecosystem.

Two Ways to Contribute:
1. Native Windows Daemon (Recommended): Download KContribute.exe (159 KB). It sits in your Windows System Tray, auto-starts on login, and never distracts you.
2. Web Dashboard: Keep this page open in your browser to run simulated or live work units right inside KiloOS.
