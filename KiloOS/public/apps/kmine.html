💣 KMine - Rules & Keyboard Shortcuts

Objective: Uncover all safe tiles across the grid without detonating any hidden naval mines. Numbers indicate how many adjacent mines touch that tile.

🖱️ Mouse Controls
  • Left-Click: Reveal tile (safe first click guaranteed)
  • Right-Click: Place or remove mine flag 🚩
  • Left-Click on Revealed Number: Chord — instantly reveals all adjacent unflagged tiles if the number of placed flags matches the number!
