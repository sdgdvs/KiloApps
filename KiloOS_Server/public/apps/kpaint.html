Drawing & Features
  • Quick Starters: 1-click presets for Demo Art (Mountain Sunset), Pixel Grid, Flowcharts, and 8-bit Sprites.
  • Drawing Tools: Pick any tool from the sidebar or press its shortcut. Adjust brush size and square/round shapes.
  • Color Palette: Select from 4 curated palettes or choose custom colors. Press I or Alt+Click to sample any color.
  • Layers: Add transparent layers, re-order, toggle visibility, and merge layers down. All drawing tools and filters target the active layer.
  • Selection: Use Magic Wand to auto-select contiguous color regions, or Lasso for freeform selection. Press Esc to clear selection.
  • Filters & Transforms: Invert colors, grayscale, brightness, contrast, edge detect, sharpen, and rotate/flip.
  • Export: Export your art directly to PNG, WEBP, or standard 24-bit BMP, or save to the KiloOS filesystem. Drag-and-drop any image onto canvas to load.
