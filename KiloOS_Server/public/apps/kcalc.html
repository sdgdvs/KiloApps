KCalc Pro — User Guide & Shortcuts

Overview & Calculator Modes

KCalc Pro provides 7 comprehensive calculation environments: Scientific Keypad (with Hyperbolic Trig & Combinatorics), Financial Calculators (PMT, FV, Margin, CAGR), Statistics & Regression suite, Calculus & Equation Solver (Simpson's 1/3 integration, differentiation, Newton-Raphson root finder, Quadratic & Cubic solvers), Matrix & Vector suite (2x2/3x3 matrix algebra, determinant, inverse, transpose, eigenvalues, 3D vector ops), Scientific Constants library, and calculation History Tape (with printable thermal paper receipt roll & Markdown export).

Welcome to KCalc Pro

High-precision scientific, financial, and statistical calculation suite.

1. Mode Navigation: Switch between Scientific, Financial (PMT/FV/Margin/CAGR), Statistics (1-Var & LinReg), Constants, and History Tape via tabs or 1–5.
2. Natural Formula Input: Type expressions directly in the fx bar (e.g. sin(45) + sqrt(144) or 10 mod 3) or click the keypad.
3. Workspace Quicksave: Press F5 anytime to quicksave your workspace, registers, and tape. Press F9 to instantly restore.
4. Clipboard & Export: Click the main display or press Ctrl+C to copy results. Export tape logs to CSV, TXT, or JSON.
